Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Rockford

Routine maintenance is the difference between a system that lasts and one that quits in the middle of the season. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a new filter every one to three months, at least two feet of open space around the outdoor condenser, a gentle rinse each spring for the condenser coil, and two professional tune-ups a year, so small issues get caught early.

Take a look at the age of your system. That number tells you a lot. Most well-maintained air conditioners reach 12 to 15 years, gas furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is beyond that point and also shows rising bills, uneven temperatures, or repeated repairs, it's usually due for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and less stressful than a rushed emergency install in peak season.

The sooner you call about a comfort problem, the smaller the repair tends to be. A capacitor that's starting to fail costs little to replace, yet the same part, ignored through a full season, can ruin the compressor and turn a simple fix into a full system replacement.
