Bolingbrook HVAC specializes in food-service HVAC, including rooftop package units, split systems, correct tonnage and airflow sizing, makeup air balancing against hood exhaust, and scheduled service that works around your hours.

Safe Installation

Professional Installation and Documentation

Every commercial job we take on starts with safety. We inspect heat exchangers for cracks, confirm correct venting and combustion air, test electrical connections and disconnects, verify the refrigerant charge, protect against condensate overflow, and measure ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is simple: no carbon monoxide hazards, no poor air quality, no refrigerant leaks, and no ceiling tiles ruined by an overflowing drain. You receive a written report with priority levels and specific recommended actions.

Regular professional service protects the people and property you're responsible for, and following through on our recommendations helps keep small issues from becoming liabilities. It also builds a paper trail. We keep service logs, temperature and filter records, and a binder of reports for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms. That record shows landlords, insurers, and auditors that you've done your due diligence.
