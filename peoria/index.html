Two situations call for quick action from you before our technician gets there. The second is a furnace that short-cycles or produces a burning smell. Shut it down at the switch and call us. Those few minutes can save you from a far bigger repair.

Keeping Your Peoria HVAC System Running Longer

Many mid-season breakdowns trace back to maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are easy. Swap your filter every one to three months, clear vegetation at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and rinse the condenser coil every spring. Then book a professional tune-up twice a year, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

The age of your equipment matters just as much. With regular service, central air typically lasts 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts showing warning signs, like higher energy bills, rooms that never reach the setpoint, or repeat service calls, it's smart to start planning a replacement. A planned replacement is cheaper and is far less stressful than an emergency swap during the worst heat wave of summer.

Minor issues rarely stay small when they're ignored. Consider a weak capacitor: swapping it out is a short, inexpensive repair, but left alone for a season, it can burn out the compressor, and suddenly a minor fix has become a full system replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to keep our number in their phone and call at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves money and prevents a few miserable nights.

Typical Heating & Cooling Issues in Peoria

Most local HVAC failures give warning signs before they happen. A breaker that trips more than once, a condenser that hums louder each month, rooms drifting further from the thermostat setting, energy bills creeping up with nothing else changed. These are the early symptoms of failing capacitors, refrigerant leaks, dirty coils, and duct problems, and caught early they're an affordable repair. Left alone, they take out compressors and heat exchangers, the two most expensive parts in the system. We diagnose the cause, not just the symptom, so small problems stay small.
